# Provenance of loci added in EC-K-Typing v4.0.0

This document records the source relationships for the 19 reference loci
added in EC-K-Typing v4.0.0.

The EC-K-Typing identifiers KL176–KL191 were newly assigned to
sequence-defined loci without established K phenotypes. The kTYPr catalogue
labels below are included only to document provenance and should not be
interpreted as EC-K-Typing locus identifiers or confirmed K phenotypes.

Each sequence was verified as a full-length exact nucleotide match (100%
identity and coverage) to its record in `kTYPrDB_for_Kaptive.fasta`. The
sequences were subsequently annotated and curated using the EC-K-Typing
workflow.
